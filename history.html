French History

The History

Like Most other nations, France came a long way to be what it is today. Going way back, the land was once conquered by Gauls, a
Celtic tribe. This went on until 52BCE, when Julius Caesar took over Gaul and beought Roman culture, cities, and roads to the place.

The Middle Ages

During the middle ages, France became one of the most powerful kingdoms in Europe. Over this period, amazing castles, churches, and
cathedrals were built. When the Hundred Years' War began, the French played a major role in the fight against England.

The French Revolution

Jumping over to the French Revolution in 1789, there was a monarchy that overpowered French citizens, imposing high taxes and limiting their freedoms.
This demand for freedom was what sparked the revolution, which eventually ended the Monarchy. To this day, the storming of the Bastille
prison on July 14, 1789 is celebrated as Bastille Day, and it is France's national holiday.
