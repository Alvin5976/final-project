Chicken Sandwich

  • Time: 18 minutes
  • Serves: 1
Ingredients Quantity
bread 2
Chicken 1
lettuce 2
Mayonesa 1

Step 1: Get two slices of bread and cook them for 2 minutes

Step 2: Cook a piece of chicken for 15 minutes

Step 3: When finished cooking, place the chicken in between the bread slices, and add lettuce to it.

Step 4: No add mayo and enjoy

Fruit soup

  • Time: 35 minutes
  • Serves: 6
Ingredients Quantity
Apples 5
Plaintains 4
Papayas 4
Coconuts 4
Water 1 gallon

Step 1: Chop every ingredient(but water) into bits

Step 2: Put the fruit and water into a tall pot

Step 3: Cook for 30 minutes

Step 4: When done, allow it time to cool and Enjoy!

Pupusa

  • Time: 12 minutes
  • Serves: 2
Ingredients Quantity
Masa dough 15 oz
oil 1 cup
cheese 1 oz

Step 1: Heat up the cheese until it softens

Step 2: Put the heated cheese onto some masa

Step 3: Soak hands with oil, and roll masa to cover the cheese

Step 4: Flatten the masa into a circle shape

Step 5: Cook the pupusa for 10 minuites

Step 4: Let the pupusa cool then enjoy!
