The Mona Lisa

Basic facts

The Mona Lisa: an iconic work of art painted by italian artist, Leonardo da Vinci between 1503 and 1506. Today, it is one
of the world's most famous paintings. After Leonardo's death, this oil painting became part of the Frecnh Royal collection,
and now it is displayed in the louvre Museum, making it one of France's greatest cultural attractions
