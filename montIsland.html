Mont saint-Michel island

Basic facts

The island of Mont Saint-Michel is a small rocky island off the coast
of Normandy, in northwestern France. It is highlighted by its medival
abbey, which sits at the highest point of the island. This place is connected to
the mainland by a causeway, allowing visits get there by walking! For what its got, Mont
Saint-Michel became a UNESCO World Heritage site in 1979, becoming recognized for its
medival architecture and historical importance.

Around the Island

There are amazing tides of water surrounding this island.In fact, Mont Saint-Michel
is famous for having the highest tidesin Europe. During high tide
conditions, the island appears to float as it is surrounded by water. On the flip side,
low tides allow visitors to walk across the surrounding sand flats, when they are with a guide of course.
