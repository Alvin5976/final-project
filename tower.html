The Eiffel Tower

Basic Information

The Eiffel Tower, located in Paris, France, was completed in 1889 for the
World's Fair, which celebrated 100 years since the French Revolution. It was
designed by Gustave Eiffel, and engineer whose company built it.

Fun Facts

  1. It was built for the 1889 World's Fair to celebrate the 100th anniversary of the French Revolution.
  2. The tower is about 330 meters (1,083 feet) tall, making it one of the tallest structures in France.
  3. It was not supposed to remain. the Eiffel Tower was supposed to be a temporary structure and nearly got taken down after 20 years.
